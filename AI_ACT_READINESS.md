# DETERMA for AI Act Readiness

## Important boundary

DETERMA does not claim to make any organization compliant with the EU AI Act by itself.

Compliance requires legal, organizational, technical, and operational work.

DETERMA may support AI Act readiness by providing technical controls and runtime evidence for AI-initiated actions.

## The readiness problem

Organizations preparing for AI governance obligations need to demonstrate more than policy intent.

They need evidence of:
- oversight
- traceability
- accountability
- risk control
- execution boundaries
- auditability

As AI systems become more agentic, organizations will need to show that AI-triggered actions are controlled at runtime, not only reviewed in documents.

## Where DETERMA fits

DETERMA is designed to help organizations convert written AI governance policies into runtime-enforced controls.

A DETERMA-style execution flow can support:
- human approval as a hard stop
- separation between proposal and execution
- scoped execution authority
- pre-execution state checks
- constrained executors
- post-execution verification
- immutable evidence trails

## Public framing

Use:

> DETERMA supports AI Act readiness by creating runtime evidence and enforceable execution controls for AI-initiated actions.

Do not use:

> DETERMA guarantees AI Act compliance.

## Why this matters

Regulatory governance becomes stronger when it is backed by operational evidence.

DETERMA is built for that evidence layer.
